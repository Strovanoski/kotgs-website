For Justice, Honor, and the Glory of the Golden Spoon!!

Formed in the dawn of online worlds and reborn in Monsters & Memories. We are a family bound by legacy, epic adventures, and a touch of golden whimsy.

The Oath of the Golden Spoon

"We swear to hold aloft the Spoon in times of shadow. We shield the weary, feed the crafting forges, and face down the deep horrors of this realm. Together as one family, we stand."

Our fellowship was forged in the ancient, historical era of Sierra's The Realm Online (1996). In those days, we learned that community, honor, and mutual aid were the true treasures of any world.

Now, as the world of Monsters & Memories wakes up, we return. Our goal is not just conquest, but the nurturing of a home for players who value genuine companionship, steady PvE progress, master craftsmanship, and unwavering chivalry. We do not tolerate toxic pursuits—only the legendary justice of the Spoon.
